# Changelog

The nuts and bolts of every version are in the
[detailed changelog](https://github.com/lowlightworks/Sanity/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG-detailed.md).

## 0.0.3 (2026-11-07)

### New
- Phantom hits now hit like the real thing. Heard a thumper behind you? That's a thumper-sized hit, and a
  thumper-sized scare.
- Pulling the apparatus gets to you now: the lights die, your nerves take a hit, and the dark facility wears you down
  faster on the way out.
- Anonymous gameplay stats, to help balance the mod. You're told on the main menu and can switch it off with one
  click. No names, no IDs; everything that's sent is listed in the README.
- A feedback form and a Discussions page: tell us what got you (links in the README).

### Changed
- Locked doors and vanishing loot turn up earlier, and a little more often.

### Fixed
- Fixed a few bugs, including lost time showing up too early in the day.

## 0.0.2 (2026-11-06)

### Changed
- Sounds and footsteps no longer get lost while you sprint: they wait until you slow down enough to hear them.
- Lost time reworked: you step out of the facility and it's night, music cue and all. It happens more often, never
  right in front of you, and sometimes your walkie clicks on right after.

## 0.0.1 (2026-11-06)

First public release. Very early and still rough: it's out so it's easy to install and share, not because it's
finished. Expect rough edges, and balance changes between versions.

### New
- A hidden sanity meter for every player. Stay alone in the dark too long and it starts slipping; the ship, your crew
  and a good haul bring you back.
- Things start happening that only you can see and hear: lights, sounds, footsteps, a door that won't open... and
  something that likes to follow you around.
- Plays nice with popular mods: HUD mods, Lategame Upgrades, weather mods and modded moons.
- Settings to turn any of it down, or off.
